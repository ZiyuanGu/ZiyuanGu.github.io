## Research areas

My research focuses on the modelling, simulation, optimisation, and intelligent management of complex transportation systems. My current research programme comprises four interconnected areas.

<div class="research-grid">
<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### 1. Multi-fidelity transportation network modelling

Development of multi-fidelity, integrated macro–micro, and agent-based models for analysing transportation systems at different spatial and operational scales.

**Key topics:** multi-fidelity models · integrated macro–micro modelling · agent-based simulation · large-scale network analysis
</div>
<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### 2. Simulation and optimisation of complex transportation systems

Development of simulation and intelligent optimisation methods for complex and emerging transportation systems, including vehicle–road–cloud and low-altitude transportation applications.

**Key topics:** simulation-based optimisation · reinforcement learning · transportation digital twins · vehicle–road–cloud systems · low-altitude transportation
</div>
<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### 3. Management and control of mixed-autonomy networks

Modelling and optimisation of transportation networks in which human-driven, connected, and automated vehicles interact.

**Key topics:** mixed-autonomy networks · connected and automated vehicles · intelligent traffic control · parking management and pricing
</div>
<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### 4. Large-language-model-enabled intelligent decision-making

Application of large language models, artificial intelligence, and parallel computing to transportation modelling, simulation, and decision-making.

**Key topics:** large language models · AI-enabled mobility · parallel computing · large-scale simulation and optimisation
</div>
</div>
