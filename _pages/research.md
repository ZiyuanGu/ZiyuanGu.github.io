Research areas
======

My research focuses on the modelling, simulation, optimisation, and intelligent management of complex transportation systems. The following four areas reflect my current research programme.

### Multi-fidelity transportation network modelling

- Multi-fidelity transportation models
- Integrated macro–micro traffic modelling
- Agent-based transportation simulation
- Large-scale transportation network analysis

### Simulation and optimisation of complex transportation systems

- Simulation-based optimisation
- Reinforcement learning
- Transportation digital twins
- Vehicle–road–cloud transportation systems
- Emerging low-altitude transportation systems

### Management and control of mixed-autonomy networks

- Mixed human-driven and automated traffic
- Connected and automated transportation systems
- Intelligent traffic management and control
- Parking network modelling, management, and pricing

### Large-language-model-enabled intelligent decision-making

- Large language models for transportation systems
- AI-enabled mobility modelling and decision-making
- Parallel computing
- Large-scale transportation simulation and optimisation

Research environment and collaboration
======

I serve as Deputy Director of the Complex Transportation Networks Research Centre and Deputy Director of the Huawei–Southeast University Joint Laboratory for Integrated Macro–Micro Traffic Simulation.

My research brings together transportation engineering, operations research, data science, artificial intelligence, mathematical modelling, and high-performance computing.
