I am an Associate Professor and PhD Supervisor in the School of Transportation at Southeast University, China. I currently serve as Deputy Director of the Complex Transportation Networks Research Centre and Deputy Director of the Huawei–Southeast University Joint Laboratory for Integrated Macro–Micro Traffic Simulation.

I received my PhD in Civil and Environmental Engineering from the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia, where I worked with Professor Meead Saberi and Professor S. Travis Waller. After completing my PhD, I remained at UNSW as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow for two years. I received my master's degree in Transportation Engineering from the Southeast University–Monash University Joint Graduate School and my bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from Nanjing Tech University.

My research focuses on the modelling, simulation, optimisation, and intelligent management of complex and emerging transportation systems. My current interests include multi-fidelity transportation network modelling, simulation-based optimisation, mixed-autonomy traffic networks, transportation digital twins, reinforcement learning, large language models, parallel computing, and AI-enabled mobility systems.

I have published more than 60 journal and conference papers. My research has appeared in *Nature Communications*, *The Innovation*, *Research*, *Transportation Science*, *IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering*, *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems*, *Transportation Research Parts A–E*, *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering*, and other leading international journals and conferences.

I have led competitive research projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Natural Science Foundation of Jiangsu Province. I have also contributed to major research and industry projects involving integrated macro–micro traffic simulation, transportation digital twins, mixed-autonomy networks, intelligent transport systems, parking management, and low-carbon mobility.

My teaching includes Transportation Big Data Analytics, Advanced Operations Research, and Data Analysis and Modelling. Transportation Big Data Analytics has been recognised as a National First-Class Undergraduate Course in China. I supervise PhD, master's, and undergraduate students from transportation engineering, operations research, management science, computer science, data science, and applied mathematics.

Prospective students
======

I welcome enquiries from motivated PhD, master's, and undergraduate students interested in mathematical modelling, traffic simulation, optimisation, artificial intelligence, and machine learning for smart transportation.

Applicants from transportation engineering, civil engineering, operations research, management science, computer science, data science, applied mathematics, and related disciplines are encouraged to contact me by email. Opportunities may also be available for international collaboration, joint supervision, and research visits.
