--- layout: archive title: "Teaching & Supervision" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} ## Courses - Transportation Big Data Analytics — National First-Class Undergraduate Course - Advanced Operations Research - Data Analysis and Modelling ## Prospective students I recruit 1–2 PhD students each year, including candidates for joint PhD programmes with Monash University, Australia, and 2–3 master’s students each year, including students based at Southeast University’s Wuxi campus. Applicants should have strong mathematical and computing skills. There is no restriction on disciplinary background; relevant fields include, but are not limited to, transportation, management science, computer science and mathematics. Opportunities are available for academic exchange, joint training and visiting study in China and overseas. Undergraduate students are also welcome to participate in Student Research Training Program (SRTP) projects and transportation science and technology competitions. ## Graduate outcomes ### Master’s graduates, Class of 2025 - Yang Cao — China Development Bank, Hunan Branch - Guiqi Dai — Huawei Markov Laboratory
